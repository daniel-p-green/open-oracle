How Open Oracle works
  1. 01
    Ask in your own words.
    Start with a measure or candidate question. This demo uses retained sources for the election shown above.
  2. 02
    Keep the original in view.
    Read an explanation beside exact ballot wording, or compare attributed candidate answers to the same question.
  3. 03
    Follow the evidence.
    Open the source. See what is missing. Open Oracle does not choose your vote or verify every candidate claim.
In live research mode, source and review checks must pass before an explanation is released. The local retained-source mode does not run AI review. Readability estimates describe wording, not factual accuracy or legal meaning.
