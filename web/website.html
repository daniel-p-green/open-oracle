What happens before an AI explanation is shown?

Live research must read the scoped sources and pass source-binding, factual and independent review checks before releasing an explanation. A failed check produces a gap or a held answer. These checks are fallible. The retained-source demo can run without live AI review and labels that mode.

Readability diagnostics can describe dense wording. They do not establish accuracy, comprehension or legal meaning. Private PDF/TXT/paste preview and an explicitly chosen English formula estimate are available when this local server enables intake. Source verification, uploaded AI explanations, translation and continuous voice are not connected.
