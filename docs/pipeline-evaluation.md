## Final WCAG acceptance

`evals/wcag-release-matrix.json` inventories all 55 WCAG 2.2 A/AA criteria, with applicability and evidence unassessed. The proposed final target is [WCAG 2.2 AA web content](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). As [W3C evaluation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/) explains, tools assist evaluation; a complete evaluation needs human judgment. No scan or static check here establishes conformance.

Bind evidence to the final build, complete pages and full process states on the 16:9 TV, desktop and both iPad orientations. Run automated accessibility/contrast/semantics checks, then manual keyboard-only question/source/style/language/Play/Stop/Resume/Erase flow; focus visibility/order/return and obscuration; VoiceOver announcements and page/part language; text before/during sound without duplicate screen-reader speech; applicable captions/media alternatives; contrast including focus/control states; 200% text zoom and 400%/320 CSS-pixel reflow; text spacing; touch targets/spacing and pointer cancellation; motion/gesture alternatives; error and permission-denial recovery. Determine media criteria applicability from the actual media, not the name “live.”

Reduced motion is an additional product requirement related to AAA 2.3.3; do not mislabel it AA. [Target-size minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) includes size/spacing exceptions that require assessment of the rendered targets. Record a rationale for every not-applicable criterion. Physical reach, seated view/distance, glare, intelligibility, microphone behavior and hardware Stop/Erase are a separate physical-device matrix. This work provides no legal compliance assurance.
