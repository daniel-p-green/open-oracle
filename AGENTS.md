# Open Oracle project guidance

Open Oracle helps voters understand official ballot wording and attributed candidate statements. It never ranks candidates, endorses measures or tells voters how to vote. Current scope is November 3, 2026, Jackson County, Missouri, covering KCEB and JCEB; historical sources are separate evaluation data.

## Work and ownership

- Inspect current files, running work and applicable instructions before editing. Prefer small changes to existing components; avoid duplicate controllers or parallel monoliths.
- Core owns evidence/release logic, provider orchestration, cancellation and repository Git mutations. Coordinate staged imports by hashes and reviewed handoffs. Desktop/Pi interaction belongs to its assigned owner.
- The design owner owns root DESIGN.md. The new america.gov direction is authorized for the design owner’s isolated preview. Keep active visual files stable until its reviewed patch is coordinated; earlier rejected concepts remain held.
- Preserve research originals and their provenance. Local Codex memories are historical; do not update them unless requested.

## Source and release contracts

- Keep election date, jurisdiction, authority, source role/version and candidate identity application-owned. Clarify missing jurisdiction; never infer personal ballot eligibility.
- Retain exact original wording, hashes and offsets. Candidate statements are attributed claims. OCR, missing sources, historical editions, conflicting versions and proposed/current-law distinctions must be explicit.
- Strands must execute substantive scoped retrieval/tools; do not wrap a finished deterministic answer and call that agent research. Avoid unnecessary agents/swarms.
- Research drafts remain private until the claim/source/neutrality/jurisdiction/language release adapter has been validated. Citation presence and operational receipts are not quality approval.
- Decisions checks are fallible and thresholds uncalibrated. Preserve deterministic source/numeric/display guards, abstention, and independent checks. Readability scores are diagnostics, never factual-fidelity, comprehension or voting-advice gates.
- Separate canonical spoken transcript, simplified explanation and official original. Captions describe actual accepted speech. GPT-Live deltas are not character alignment; do not invent timing or infer speaking from generation. Preserve the existing manual Play and request/evidence receipt gates.

## Privacy, cancellation and slow connections

- Capture begins only after deliberate initiation and device permission. Do not start ambient capture or sound while the user is away. Stop/Erase must cancel pending work, clear local visitor content and reject late display/audio.
- No raw visitor transcript/audio/video or tool/model contents in operational receipts. Default debug storage/exporters remain off; aggregate counters omit visitor identifiers and source history. Local erase cannot unsend or promise provider deletion.
- Use retained, hash-checked local evidence and locally served assets for the typed fallback. Cloud inference/review/voice require a connection; never claim they work offline. Keep timeouts, bounded work and cancellation; never silently retry paid requests.

## SDLC and approvals

- Make coherent tested local commits regularly, with meaningful problem/behavior messages and relevant validation/log references. Inspect staged diffs for secrets, unrelated work and generated artifacts; do not use git add-all blindly.
- Run checks proportionate to the change. Preserve the tested typed path, source/advice/voice gates and erase isolation. Record actual passes, skips, failures and unrun live/device checks; never label authored model events as live inference.
- Live Open Oracle API testing has a shared $100 total authorization, not unlimited spend. Coordinate/reserve bounded trials in the local shared ledger before running; preserve no-retry bounds and report estimates separately from invoices.
- Do not extract/store secrets, create credentials, change persistent authentication, deploy, publish, push, launch billable resources or send external messages without the applicable authorization. Existing permitted integrations remain within their permissions.
- Keep .env, credentials, .aws, .local, environments and full research archive out of Git. Local commits do not authorize pushing or publication.
- Target the first three-minute video export around 5–6am Central on October 11, leaving upload verification before 8am and the separate 9am live demo.
